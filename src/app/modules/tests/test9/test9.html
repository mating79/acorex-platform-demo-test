Responsive Breakpoint Test

Tests per-breakpoint position persistence. Each widget stores independent x/y/w/h for lg (12 cols), md (6 cols), and sm (1 col). Drag widgets at one breakpoint, switch to another, and verify positions are preserved when you switch back. How to test
1. Arrange widgets at lg breakpoint
2. Click md button — positions should load from md profile, NOT auto-reflow from lg
3. Drag a widget at md
4. Click lg — positions should be exactly as you left them in step 1
5. Click md — the drag from step 3 should be preserved
6. Try sm too. Each breakpoint is fully independent.
